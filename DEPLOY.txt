LEXICON QUEST: SHARE WITH FAMILY

1. Download and unzip lexicon_quest_mobile_web.zip on a computer.
2. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop and drag the unzipped lexicon_quest_web folder onto the page.
3. Netlify will return an HTTPS URL. Open the URL in Safari on an iPhone or Chrome on Android.
4. Share the URL by text or email. Each person starts their own chronicle, with 3 browser save slots.
5. iPhone: Safari Share -> Add to Home Screen. Android: Chrome menu -> Add to Home screen / Install app.

IMPORTANT
- Browser saves remain on each device and browser; clearing website data deletes them. They do not sync across devices.
- The ZIP itself is not an app-store package, and the file cannot be launched reliably in the iOS Files preview.
- Publishing requires a Netlify account or another static hosting provider. The upload is not performed automatically by ChatGPT.
- This is a playable eight-chapter campaign implementation, not a finished commercial 15-20 hour RPG; future production work is needed for art, music, content depth, cross-device sync, and comprehensive testing.
