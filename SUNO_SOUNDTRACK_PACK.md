## Integration notes

The runtime supports separate music/effects volume, master mute, automatic location and battle selection, crossfades, one-shot victory/rest/defeat cues, and synthesized fallback. Playback waits for a click, tap, or key press. Leaving the browser pauses playback. Imported music is checked for MP3 filename/type, a 25 MB size limit, and decodable duration. A rejected upload does not overwrite an existing track.

Loops currently use the whole exported file. Gapless musical edits and loudness matching need the actual exports and listening tests. Browser/phone listening remains pending.
