# Sprites (Lexicon Quest NEXT)

Generated by `next/tools/art/sprites/build_all.py` (deterministic, about 10 seconds). Do not hand-edit the PNGs:
change the generator (hand-pixeled grids live in `tools/art/sprites/chars/*.py`) and rebuild. Colours come from
`next/assets/palette.json` at build time (legends use `ramp:index` refs), so a palette tweak only needs a rebuild.
Contact sheets at 4x and mock screens are written to `tools/art/sprites/out/`.

All atlases follow DESIGN 4: `{"image", "frames": {name: [x,y,w,h]}, "anims": {name: {frames, fps, loop}}}`
plus the extra keys noted below. Draw with nearest-neighbour at integer positions.

## party_field.png / .json  (24x32 cells, origin [12,31] = feet)
Per character a 4-row block: rows `down, left, right, up`; columns `stand, stepA, stepB, blink`.
Frame names `<id>_<dir>_<0|1|2|blink>`; anims `<id>_walk_<dir>` (A, stand, B, stand at 8 fps) and
`<id>_idle_<dir>` (stand with an occasional blink). Ids: `hero`, `mira`, `bram`. Right = mirrored left.

## party_battle.png / .json  (32x40 cells, facing LEFT, origin [19,39] = feet)
Weapons per character: hero: sword, axe; mira: staff, bow; bram: spear, axe.
Frames: `<id>_<weapon>_{idle_0,idle_1,ready,windup,strike,victory}` for each of its weapons, and
`<id>_{cast,hurt,defend,kneel,ko}` (kneel = low HP). Anims `<id>_<weapon>_idle` (2-frame breathe) and
`<id>_<weapon>_attack` (ready, windup, strike). Weapon arcs, flashes and numbers are code/fx.

## props.png / .json
`objects.<id>`: `frame [x,y,w,h]`, `anchor [px,py]` (bottom-centre of the footprint inside the frame),
`footprint [wTiles,hTiles]`, `solid`, `overheadY` (frame rows above it draw over actors, 0 = none), optional
`frames`/`frameNames` (state alternates), `passable` tiles, `breaksTo`. Placement: anchor = bottom-centre of the footprint in frame px. To place an object whose footprint bottom-centre sits at tile (tx,ty): draw frame at x = tx*16 + footprint[0]*8 - anchor[0] (tx = left footprint tile), y = ty*16 + 15 - anchor[1] (ty = bottom footprint row). overheadY: frame rows [0, overheadY) draw in the overhead layer above actors (0 = none). frames/frameNames: state alternates (closed/open, unlit/lit, glint).

  `tree_willow`, `tree_pine`, `tree_pine_snow`, `tree_violet`, `fountain`, `arch_ruin`
  `rune_shrine`, `lamp_post`, `bench`, `anvil`, `hedge_flowers`, `bush`
  `bookshelf`, `desk`, `bed`, `fireplace`, `printing_press`, `lectern`
  `crates_barrels`, `rug`, `barrel`, `crate`, `pot`, `pot_broken`
  `signboard`, `prop_milestone`, `prop_thirteenth_bell`, `prop_letter`, `prop_ferry_bell`, `chest_wood`
  `chest_iron`, `chest_gold`, `lamp_glow`

Chests: `chest_wood|iron|gold` with frames [closed, open]. `lamp_post` frames [unlit, lit]; `lamp_glow` is an
additive glow disc. `prop_letter` frames [plain, glint]. `pot` breaks to `pot_broken`.

## enemies_battle.png / .json, bosses_battle.png / .json  (face RIGHT, origin = bottom-centre)
enemies_battle: `ink_slime`, `page_wraith`, `briar_wolf`, `moss_scarab`, `bookmite`, `tome_golem`, `ink_wraith`, `thornback_wolf`, `ink_blot`, `briar_alpha`.
bosses_battle: `archivore`, `echo_warden`, `lost_page_0`, `lost_page_1`, `lost_page_2`, `lost_page` (anim `lost_page_flutter`).
Derived frames (W6-anim, `anim_frames.py`): `<id>_idle_1`, `_attack`, `_hurt`, `_cast` (casters), `_devour`
(Archivore), `_die_0..2`, Echo Warden stance sets; anims `<id>_idle` and `<id>_die`. Every frame has its own origin.
Whisperwood Hollow (W8-art, `hollow_enemies.py`): `thornback_wolf` (old silver-violet fur, black-green bramble, bone
thorns), `ink_blot` (tar-black ink, cyan eyes, puddle and drips, rim-lit) and `briar_alpha` (a clean 1x larger wolf
from the painting: warm brown fur, briar mane, gold-tipped thorns, rose hips) have their own base and derived frames,
packed in a block under the older frames (no older rect moved). Draw them at 1x with no tint.
`enemies_battle.json.external` maps the boss-sheet ids so battle code can look up any enemy id.
Cleanup pass (CRITIQUE_1 C8, `cleanup.py`): colours within 0.04 OKLab of a master colour snap to it, near-duplicate
colours merge, isolated pixels take their majority neighbour (bright saturated accents are protected), and the 1 px
outer outline uses the darkest step of the ramp each neighbouring material belongs to (never universal black).
Dark sprites on the dark stage (CRITIQUE_2 item 4, W5-art): `bookmite`, `page_wraith`, `ink_wraith` and the
Archivore's lower body carry a 1 px rim light on their upper-left edges (`cleanup.rim_light`: a lighter step of
their own ramp, ink:4 on the ink wraiths and the mite, hair_violet:4 on the Archivore) and their darkest fill
lifted one ramp step (`cleanup.lift_darkest`; the outline stays the ramp's darkest). Frame rects are unchanged.
Judge them on the stages with `tools/art/backdrops/mock_stage.py` (out/mock_stage_<setup>_3x.png).
Recolours: page_wraith and bookmite are parchment/ink gradient maps, briar_wolf brown fur + green briar,
ink_wraith ink-violet. Idle motion, hit flash and death dissolve are procedural (DESIGN 6.3).

## ../ui/heads.png / .json  (timeline head icons, 12x12)
`head_<id>` for hero, mira, bram and every enemy and boss id: a 10x10 head on `#251d34` inside a 1 px `#14101e` frame.
