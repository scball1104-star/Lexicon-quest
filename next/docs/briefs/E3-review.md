# E3-review: critic-5 and play-critic-5 (wave 17e, read-only, one at a time)

Two read-only reviews of the published Episode 3, run one after the other, then the orchestrator writes the E3-fixes
brief from them. Read `docs/briefs/COMMON.md`, `DESIGN.md` sections 1 and 10, `docs/story/EPISODE3.md`,
`docs/EPISODE3_PLAN.md` 12 and 13, and the format of `docs/CRITIQUE_4.md` and `docs/PLAYTEST_REVIEW_4.md`. Tag claims
Fact, Reported or Inference.

## critic-5 (`docs/CRITIQUE_5.md`)

Art and UX on the E3-integrate shots against Chrono Trigger, Lufia II and FF6, the phone as the bar: does ash read as
ash (the tint), do the three foundry floors look different, do sluices and troughs read at rest, are field foes
visible on ash, do Heat pips and the Oath read in battle, is the Warden a readable shape. Fifteen findings ranked by
player impact with sizes, and a protect list.

## play-critic-5 (`docs/PLAYTEST_REVIEW_5.md`)

A new player on a phone, from an Episode 2 save and from the quick start: pacing (the first fight within five minutes;
the timer against 60 to 90), clarity of every quench puzzle without text, whether quench feels different from the
beams, difficulty of the Specter and the Warden, whether the Oath decision is discovered, the save rule (try to lose a
save; you should not be able to). Ten to fix, ten to protect.

## Rules

- Owned paths: your review file and scratch output in `qa/out/review5/` only. Read anything; change nothing else.
- No git, no em dashes, bounded commands, one browser job at a time, short headless sessions.
- Report under 400 words: the verdict, the top five items with sizes, and what not to touch.
