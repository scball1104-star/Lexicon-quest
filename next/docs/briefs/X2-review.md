## Items, in order

1. **Pacing and levels**: the main route is unchanged (confirm the King and the Other Selves at today's levels). Does the optional world read as paying in gold, gear and items rather than levels? Is a completionist at most about a level over the story, and are the four optional bosses fair at the stated levels? Is the first fight still early?
2. **The map and the routes**: do the Waystation's five ways and the two loops read as choices? Can a first-time player find each branch (the stairs, the toll bridge, the Hollow's two gates, the second boat, the hatch) and each secret's tell without Talk? Are the big fields rewarding to cross (landmarks, far-off views, hidden paths) or just long? Time one completionist run
   against plan 16.
3. **Clarity**: is Talk always naming the next leg of each quest? Is any quest a dead end for a save from a given point? Does the small quest EXP read as a bonus? Do Strangers' Stores and the Episode 1 quests (Pell's Crates, The Thirteenth Stone) read as different from each other?
4. **Art and UX on a phone**: boardwalks, ink bridges, hide planks, mirror states, the lamp, the lens bench, the Cistern's mercury, the 80 x 40 and 84 x 36 fields at phone scale; the Quarry against the Episode 1 barrow (different floors, walls, mood). Compare to Chrono Trigger, Lufia II and FF6; flag anything unreadable.
5. **Audio**: each of the nine tracks on its maps (the Weald and Southmere clearly different tunes, loop seams, mix, the motif), the optional boss theme, the SFX pack loading on the first entry.
6. **Load and saves**: the budget line; the pack failing offline; three real saves (mid-road, post-Court, post-credits) loaded into the build; every gate behaves as plan 13 says.
7. **Writing**: the six quests against the tone guide; any line that names something the player has not learned; any em dash.
8. Ten moments to protect and ten to fix, ranked by player impact, each with where, what was observed, the one change, and what must stay fixed (the four-field shape).
